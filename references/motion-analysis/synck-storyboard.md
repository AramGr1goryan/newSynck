# Project SyncK — Motion Storyboard

This document maps the visual and motion patterns from the `finzy-motion-reference.mp4` to the specific product storytelling requirements of Project SyncK.

## 01 HERO (Status: Already implemented)
* **Reference Timestamp**: 00:00 - 00:02
* **Reference Motion**: Typography fade-up, staggered UI mockup entrance from bottom.
* **SyncK Adaptation**: "Everything in sync." Huge typography, negative space, and a premium CRM UI mockup entering from the bottom with GSAP ScrollTrigger.

## 02 THE IDEA
* **Reference Timestamp**: 00:03 - 00:05 (Metrics / Stats)
* **Reference Motion**: Left-aligned heading fade-up, followed by staggered entrance of key conceptual blocks.
* **SyncK Adaptation**: Explain "What SyncK actually means". Staggered reveal of text blocks explaining the ecosystem metaphor. Clean, minimal, typographic.

## 03 SYNCK CRM
* **Reference Timestamp**: 00:05 - 00:07 (Bounded Feature Cards - Row 1)
* **Reference Motion**: Large rounded cards containing floating UI elements. Cards parallax upwards.
* **SyncK Adaptation**: Immersive presentation of CRM modules (schedules, clients, analytics). Cards will contain DOM-based UI components (tables, charts) that float independently inside the cards. 

## 04 CRM ECOSYSTEM
* **Reference Timestamp**: 00:07 - 00:08 (Bounded Feature Cards - Row 2)
* **Reference Motion**: Micro-animations inside cards (donut charts filling), seamless continuation of the card grid.
* **SyncK Adaptation**: Show modules transforming and connecting. DOM elements inside the cards will move and align to demonstrate the "Sync" concept.

## 05 MIGRATION
* **Reference Timestamp**: 00:09 - 00:10 (6-Grid Features)
* **Reference Motion**: Structured grid with staggered fade-up.
* **SyncK Adaptation**: Transition from "Excel" to "SyncK". A visual step-by-step grid or flow showing unstructured data transforming into structured CRM cards.

## 06 SYNCK TG BOT
* **Reference Timestamp**: 00:11 - 00:13 (Horizontal Testimonials)
* **Reference Motion**: Horizontal scroll tied to vertical scroll.
* **SyncK Adaptation**: The "Command -> Automation -> Result" flow works perfectly as a horizontal sequence. As the user scrolls vertically, the steps slide horizontally: Step 1 (Telegram Command) -> Step 2 (Processing) -> Step 3 (CRM Updated).

## 07 SYNCK FOR YOUR BUSINESS
* **Reference Timestamp**: 00:05 - 00:08 (Reusing card parallax)
* **Reference Motion**: Large feature cards with nested UI.
* **SyncK Adaptation**: Custom website creation. Cards representing Brief -> Design -> Dev -> Launch, with responsive website mockups floating inside them.

## 08 SYNCK ECOSYSTEM
* **Reference Timestamp**: Custom Adaptation combining layer overlap.
* **Reference Motion**: Layered convergence.
* **SyncK Adaptation**: All products (CRM, Bot, Business) converge into one visual hub. Elements from previous sections slide into a central composition.

## 09 SYNCK MOBILE
* **Reference Timestamp**: 00:13 - 00:15 (Dark App CTA)
* **Reference Motion**: Dark section slides up over the white section. Massive typography, blurred phone background.
* **SyncK Adaptation**: "Coming next". A dark, premium teaser section sliding over the ecosystem. Focus on a sleek mobile prototype.

## 10 FINAL CTA
* **Reference Timestamp**: 00:15 - 00:17 (Layered Footer)
* **Reference Motion**: Curtain reveal from underneath, huge abstract typography, floating UI.
* **SyncK Adaptation**: "Bring your business into sync." Footer reveals smoothly. Final CTA button and links.
